Our family friend's sweet potato cassorole, one of our favorites

description

A sweet potato casserole is a warm, comforting side dish with a creamy, lightly sweet filling made from mashed sweet potatoes, butter, milk, and warm spices such as cinnamon and nutmeg. It is often baked until soft and golden, then topped with a layer of toasted marshmallows, a buttery brown-sugar crumble, or crunchy pecans. Its rich, smooth texture and sweet flavor make it especially popular at holiday meals, where it pairs well with savory dishes like roasted turkey, ham, and stuffing.

ingredients

  • large can sweet potatoes
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 pound butter
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • pinch of all spice
  • 1 bag mini marshmallows

steps

  1. beat potatoes with mixer
  2. add rest
  3. pour into greased cassorole dish
  4. sprinkle on the marshmallows
  5. heat until marshmallows melted
