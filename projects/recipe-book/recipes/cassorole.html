One of our family friend's sweet potato cassorole, one of our favorites

large can sweet potatoes 3/4 cup brown sugar 1/4 pound butter 3 eggs, beaten 1/2 cup milk 1/2 teaspoon vanilla 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon pinch of all spice 1 bag mini marshmallows beat potatoes with mixer add rest pour into greased cassorole dish sprinkle on the marshmallows heat until marshmallows melted
